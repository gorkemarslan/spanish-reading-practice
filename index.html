01 / Information

Information we handle

Spanish Reading Practice does not require an account and does not ask for your name, postal address, phone number, or precise location.

The app stores learning information—such as reading progress and saved vocabulary—locally on your device. The developer does not receive this information. If you delete the app, locally stored app data may also be deleted, subject to your device and backup settings.

02 / Advertising

Advertising and third-party data

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as your device or advertising identifier, IP address, general location derived from your IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. They use this information to provide, measure, secure, and—when you consent—personalize advertising.

Where required by law, the app presents a consent message before requesting personalized ads. Google’s use of information is governed by its own policies. Learn more on Google’s advertising information page .

03 / Device services

Pronunciation and device services

The pronunciation feature uses Apple’s system speech-synthesis technology. Its operation may depend on the voices, language settings, and services available on your device. Apple’s handling of information is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy .

04 / Your choices

Your privacy choices

Where available, you can review or change your advertising privacy choices from the app’s Privacy Choices option. You can also manage relevant privacy and advertising settings in iOS.

05 / Retention

Data retention and security

Learning data stored by the app remains on your device until you remove it or delete the app. Information processed by Google or Apple is retained under their respective policies. We take reasonable steps to minimize the information the app handles, but no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06 / Updates

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features, service providers, or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this policy will be revised when an update is published.
